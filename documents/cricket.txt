Beneath the sun on emerald grass, the twenty-two yards lie,

Where wooden stumps and balanced bails stand proud against the sky.

A silver coin is tossed aloft to seal the morning’s fate,

While eager crowds fill every stand inside the stadium gate.

The bowler marks a measured run and grips the leather sphere,

With eyes fixed on the distant crease as tension fills the air.

Eleven fielders crouch in wait, a ring of watchful grace,

Prepared to dive and chase the ball across the open space.

The batter taps the willow blade and takes a steady guard,

A quiet battle of the mind inside a grassy yard.

With thunderous pace the fast man charges down the dusty track,

And hurls a gleaming red-seamed ball to push the batter back.

A crisp straight drive rings off the bat, a sound so pure and sweet,

The ball speeds past the diving hands and rolls to fans' concrete.

Four runs are signalled by the hand that sweeps across the waist,

While batters catch their breath again, no need for frantic haste.

The spinner enters to the fray with subtle flight and guile,

Deceiving eyes with drifting turns and wearing down the while.

A deceptive googly dips and spins right past the defensive blade,

And shatters timber to the roar of every fan arrayed.

Through five long days of Test match grit, true character is shown,

Where patience builds an innings brick by brick, and stone by stone.

In white attire they brave the heat, the shadows, and the dew,

Testing the limits of their skill until the fifth day's through.

Then comes the rush of fifty overs, dressed in vibrant hues,

Where powerplays and middle spells bring shifting tides and views.

Under the bright stadium lights, the white ball cuts the night,

As T20 brings explosive strokes and pure athletic might.

A scorching yorker seeks the toes to stem the scoring flow,

While batters ramp and scoop the ball with fearless, daring show.

With one ball left and six to win, a hush falls on the ground,

A million heartbeats pause as one, without a single sound.

The bowler steams in from the mark, the batter swings with might,

The ball sails high into the dark, a comet in the night.

It clears the rope into the stands, a roar shakes every tier,

As teammates rush onto the turf to celebrate and cheer.

From dusty lanes and gully games where makeshift stumps are laid,

To grand arenas of the world where legends have been made.

Through swinging bat and spinning ball, through triumph and defeat,

The spirit of this timeless game makes every pulse beat sweet.

Whether at Lord's or Wankhede, or Melbourne's mighty ring,

The magic of the willow tree makes countless voices sing.

The bails are drawn, the shadows stretch across the cooling lawn,

Until another match begins to greet the morning dawn.
